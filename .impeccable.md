## Design Context

### Users

Bryt Kids serves parents and families who want to raise children with faith, character, confidence and a lifelong love of learning. Most first visits are expected to come from social media and WhatsApp on mobile phones. The website should help those parents understand Bryt's philosophy, find suitable services or programmes, and decide whether to work with or join the brand.

### Brand Personality

Warm, wise and confident. The experience should create trust, calm, curiosity, hope and confidence while making it clear that Bryt takes children and their development seriously.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light, editorial "warm educational institution" aesthetic: premium, spacious, modern and human, with cream as the dominant surface and navy providing structure. The logo's sky blue, orange, green and pink appear as restrained accents and geometric punctuation. Use the approved hero as the visual reference: spacious navy typography, aqua actions, organic shapes and colourful line illustrations. Build curiosity through purposeful hover, focus, entrance and scroll interactions; always respect reduced motion. Avoid generic boxed cards, numbered badges as artwork, stock decoration, clutter and competing focal points.

### Design Principles

1. Less noise, more meaning; every element must earn its place.
2. Prioritise services, then programmes, library, journal and about content.
3. Treat children as capable people and parents as thoughtful decision-makers.
4. Design mobile-first for fast reading, clear navigation and generous touch targets.
5. Meet WCAG AA, preserve visible focus and zoom, and respect reduced-motion preferences.


### v1.5 component rules

- Reusable UI lives in `app/components/shared`; keep route files focused on content and composition.
- Use `DiscoveryCard` for linked services and resources, and `LearningPanel` for informational tiles. Their CSS Modules own their surfaces and interactions.
- The former `service-card`, `explore-card`, `detail-card`, numbered value tiles and plain program/topic tiles are discontinued. Do not recreate their border-top, dotted-corner, offset-shadow treatment.
- Use only the established brand palette for artwork; coral/lilac content tone names map to orange/pink artwork.
- Decorative artwork is hidden from assistive technology. Keep actions explicit, focus visible, touch targets generous and important content visible without animation or JavaScript.
- Flag newly found patterns that resemble retired designs for the owner before replacing them.

### Hero-led colour and motion

- Cards use solid, saturated brand fills inherited from the page hero: aqua, orange, pink or yellow. Avoid pale washes and gradients on cards.
- `page-theme.css` derives page tokens from the hero accent; card variants change illustration, not the page palette. Use dark navy text on these fills for readable contrast.
- Every page uses the shared `ClosingCta`: left-aligned Instrument Sans headings with a Newsreader italic accent, clear actions and animated learning doodles.
- `MotionOrchestrator` adds viewport reveals and pauses ambient artwork outside the viewport. Reduced-motion changes must take effect immediately.

### Card and background refinement

- Pair solid hero-colour cards with navy companion cards; use `card-surface.module.css` for their text, artwork and action colours.
- Use `CardTitle` for the banner-inspired sans/serif pairing. Keep body and meta text readable on each surface.
- Illustrations are transparent line art directly on the card. Do not add white blobs, discs or icon tiles behind them.
- All card collections need generous external gaps, including About beliefs and conversion paths.
- `PageDoodles` adds sparse, non-interactive margin art to content sections. Keep it clear of text, pause it off-screen and respect reduced motion.
